About Doron

A local by birth, a guide by passion

I was born in Amsterdam, a city that sparked my lifelong passion for history, culture, and meeting people from around the world.

In 2016, after completing a tour guide seminar, I made one of the best decisions of my life: becoming a professional tourist guide. Since then, I've been proud to combine my love of teaching with my passion for sharing the stories, history, and hidden gems of this beautiful city.

Whether you're visiting Amsterdam for the first time or returning to discover something new, I'd love to show you the city and the country through the eyes of a local. Book a tour with me, and let's create an unforgettable experience together!
